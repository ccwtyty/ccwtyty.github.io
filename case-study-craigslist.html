Redesign: Craigslist

A self-initiated 2-week mobile redesign — tackling Craigslist's sprawling IA, searchability problems, and the lack of in-app communication between buyers and sellers.

UX/UI Designer·Sketch · Marvel2 weeks

🎯 Problem

Craigslist's design hasn't changed since the late 1990s. Its category list is long and confusing, there's no official mobile app, and buyers and sellers communicate through external email — creating a trust gap and friction at the most critical moment of a transaction. Competitive analysis of LetGo and OfferUp showed what modern peer-to-peer marketplaces were doing right.

💡 Solution

A mobile-first redesign with three goals: simple and intuitive interface, easier search, and improved buyer-seller communication. The IA was reorganized around user intent rather than listing type, and in-app messaging was introduced to replace external email.

🧠 Key Decisions

  • Mobile-first from scratch. No official app existed — designed natively for mobile behavior and thumb-friendly navigation rather than adapting desktop.
  • Simplified category architecture. Reorganized the sprawling system around user intent rather than listing type to reduce cognitive overload.
  • In-app buyer-seller messaging. Identified communication as a key trust gap and brought messaging in-app — removing the friction of external email that was a top pain point for both parties.

📊 Impact

Results
  • Complete mobile redesign delivered in 2 weeks with clickable Marvel prototype
  • IA restructured around user intent — reduced browsing complexity
  • In-app messaging added — eliminated external email friction at transaction moment

📖 Learning

The information architecture of Craigslist was more complicated than expected — users' purposes vary wildly, and building IA around intent rather than taxonomy was the only approach that worked.
