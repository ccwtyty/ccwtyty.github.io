Sprout: Your Virtual Ecosystem

Visualize homeowners' energy consumption through a living virtual environment — where sustainable habits make the ecosystem grow, and overindulgence makes it wither.

UX/UI Designer 3D Design · Figma · Cinema 4D · After Effects

🎯 Problem

Most of the energy we consume is generated from fossil fuels — but people can't physically see the damage being done. Electric and water bills report usage without reflecting environmental impact. Smart thermostats address temperature but don't help form better habits or make the stakes feel real.

💡 Solution

A projected virtual ecosystem inside the home that responds to real-time energy and water usage. Sustainable habits = lush, dynamic environment. Overindulgence = dry, withering environment. Real-time data becomes emotionally legible through a living metaphor rather than a dashboard.

🔀 Flows

[ Home overview — virtual ecosystem + real-time alerts ]
Home OverviewSprout alerts users in real-time when there's overconsumption — showing what's consuming energy, when, and where in the home.
[ Energy consumption trends ]
Consumption TrendsUsage patterns visualized over time — know what's consuming the most so you can take action.
[ Appliance usage + alerts ]
Appliance ViewCheck per-appliance usage, set alerts if something's running too long — granular control with ambient awareness.

🧠 Key Decisions

  • Gamification over dashboards. Chose a living virtual environment metaphor instead of charts and graphs — energy data becomes personal and emotionally resonant when something alive is responding to your behavior.
  • 3D environment as behavior trigger. The virtual sprout grows or wilts based on real usage, turning abstract consumption data into a visual cue users emotionally respond to. Data alone doesn't motivate — but seeing something die because of your choices does.
  • Cross-tool execution to match concept ambition. Combined Figma for UX flows with Cinema 4D and After Effects for the 3D world — deliberately matching the tool to the scale of the idea.

🤝 Collaboration

Developed as an MFA thesis at RIT. Presented to a faculty committee for critique throughout the design process. Solo concept, research, design, and animation execution.

📊 Impact

Results
  • Full thesis project spanning UX, UI, and 3D motion design — research through polished interactive prototype
  • Demonstrated that behavior change design can be both functional and visually compelling
  • Showcased at RIT VCD Thesis Exhibition

📖 Learning

Behavior change design needs emotional hooks — data alone doesn't motivate people, but seeing something grow or die because of your choices does.
