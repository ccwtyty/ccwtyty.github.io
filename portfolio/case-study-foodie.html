Foodie: Like Me

An editorial food platform designed from scratch — daily high-quality articles, a powerful recipe search engine, and a personal Recipe Box, in a visual-first, organized layout.

UX/UI Designer·Sketch · After Effects3 weeks

🎯 Problem

Most food platforms aren't organized or clear — recipes are buried between unrelated content, and there's no single hub that brings food articles, restaurant reviews, and personal saved recipes together in one place. As a foodie, hours spent hunting across sites that aren't user-friendly was the personal frustration that started this project.

💡 Solution

Four main sections — Food, Drinks, Restaurants, Recipe Box — each with primary and secondary functions. Visual-first layout with powerful recipe search and filter, beginner-friendly video content, and a personal Recipe Box to increase return visits.

🧠 Key Decisions

  • Four-section content model. Organized around the four primary reasons users visit food sites — navigation feels instantly intuitive because it mirrors user intent.
  • Visual-first layout. Prioritized imagery and video over text — food enthusiasts browse and feel, not read.
  • Personal Recipe Box. Added a save-for-later layer to increase return visits and give users a personal stake in the platform beyond one-time browsing.

📊 Impact

Results
  • Complete editorial platform designed from scratch in 3 weeks — content strategy, IA, and interactive prototype
  • Demonstrated ability to take a personal frustration and turn it into a structured, user-centered solution

📖 Learning

Designing something you're personally passionate about is both a strength and a risk — passion drives quality, but familiarity can make it harder to see the design with fresh eyes.
