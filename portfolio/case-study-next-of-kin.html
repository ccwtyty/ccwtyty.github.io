OneDrive · Digital Legacy · Sensitive UX

Next of Kin

Balancing legal compliance with user empathy — designing OneDrive's digital legacy experience so users can designate a trusted person to access their files after death, without the friction of court orders.

Product Designer · Web Mobile Global rollout

🎯 Problem

Users had no clear way to designate a trusted person to access their OneDrive content after death — often requiring court orders that created emotional and legal friction during already difficult moments. The existing process was invisible until it was urgently needed, and the absence of a clear system left families without access to irreplaceable files and memories.

💡 Solution

Contributed to the design of the Next of Kin experience — enabling users to securely designate a beneficiary while balancing legal compliance, security, and emotional sensitivity. Used AMD to generate JSON-ready email templates, making the implementation production-ready from the first handoff.

🔀 Flows

[ Beneficiary designation setup flow ]
Beneficiary DesignationAllows users to assign access clearly and intentionally — calm, deliberate language throughout, no urgency framing.
[ Notification + confirmation email states ]
Access & NotificationCommunicates next steps respectfully and transparently — to both the designating user and the beneficiary.

🧠 Key Decisions

  • Respectful tone over technical language. Reduced legal heaviness without losing compliance accuracy. Every word was reviewed with CELA — but the goal was always clarity, not legal defensiveness.
  • Security-first access model. Flows aligned with trust and data protection standards without making users feel surveilled or mistrusted. Security needed to be strong but invisible.
  • Production-ready handoff. Used AMD to generate JSON-ready email templates directly from design, reducing implementation risk for a feature where copy precision matters legally.

🤝 Collaboration

Partnered closely with Legal (CELA), PM, Engineering, and Content Design. Translated complex legal state diagrams into clear, implementable designs. This was one of the most cross-functional projects on the team — every decision touched legal, security, and UX simultaneously.

📊 Impact

Results
  • Feature prepared for global rollout (FY25)
  • Established a secure, compliant baseline for digital legacy access at Microsoft scale
  • Reinforced user trust in OneDrive during the most sensitive moments a user can face
  • Production-ready email templates delivered directly from AMD — reduced implementation risk on legally sensitive copy

📖 Learning

Designing for grief requires slowing down, simplifying language, and making security invisible but strong — restraint is the most important design skill when the stakes are this high.
