OneDrive · OEM · Onboarding

Xiaomi SCOOBE

Designing OneDrive's out-of-box experience for Xiaomi Android devices — optimizing first-time setup and onboarding flows for a new hardware partner and a global audience.

Product Designer · Android OEM Partnership Onboarding

🎯 Problem

Xiaomi users setting up a new device for the first time had no guided path to activate and understand OneDrive — leading to low backup setup rates, missed storage permissions, and a cold start experience that felt out of place on the device. SCOOBE (Set up, Configure, Onboard, Operate, Bring up, Enable) needed to work within Xiaomi's OEM constraints while meeting Microsoft's quality bar.

💡 Solution

Designed OneDrive's out-of-box and onboarding flows specifically for Xiaomi Android devices — adapting the standard SCOOBE experience to the device's setup context, permission model, and user expectations for a hardware partner audience.

🔀 Flows

[ First-time setup — OneDrive activation on device ]
Device OOBEIntegrated into Xiaomi's setup flow — surfaces OneDrive at the moment users are making decisions about their device setup.
[ Permissions + backup setup ]
Camera Roll Backup onboardingRequests storage and camera permissions in context — explains value before asking, not after.

🧠 Key Decisions

  • Designed within OEM constraints, not around them. Xiaomi's setup flow had structural constraints that couldn't be changed. The design worked within those boundaries to still deliver a high-quality first impression.
  • Value before permission ask. Showed what OneDrive backup would do for the user before requesting camera and storage permissions — improving acceptance rates versus the ask-first pattern.
  • Localization-aware from the start. Xiaomi's global market requires designs that work across languages with very different text lengths. Layout and copy containers were designed with localization headroom built in.

🤝 Collaboration

Worked with PM and Engineering to understand Xiaomi's OEM integration requirements. Coordinated with the Permissions Optimization team to align SCOOBE onboarding with the broader Android FRE permission strategy.

📊 Impact

Results
  • Delivered OneDrive OOBE experience for Xiaomi device partnership
  • Aligned first-time setup flows with broader Android permission optimization strategy
  • Localization-ready designs reduced rework during globalization

📖 Learning

OEM partnerships expose the difference between your product's ideal experience and what's actually achievable within a partner's constraints — and designing within those constraints well is its own skill.
