Campus Mentor

A Google design challenge response — connecting mentors and mentees on campus through compatibility matching, in-app scheduling, and a community Q&A board, designed for accessibility and inclusivity.

UX/UI Designer · Figma · Photoshop Less than 1 week

🎯 Problem

Finding a mentor on campus is unnecessarily hard — students don't know where to go, setting appointments takes too long, and new students arriving at a foreign place have no connections at all. On the mentor side, reaching new students feels awkward and time-consuming without a clear channel. Five distinct pain points emerged from user interviews: lack of accessibility, discoverability, confidence, time, and connection.

💡 Solution

Three integrated features: a discovery page with compatibility scores for finding mentors, in-app scheduling with calendar and Google Maps integration, and a Reddit-style Q&A board for asynchronous community connection. Library desk reservation was also designed as part of the brief.

🔀 Flows

[ Discovery page — compatibility score cards ]
Discovery & ConnectionCard-based browsing with compatibility score — inspired by job matching and dating apps to reduce decision fatigue.
[ Scheduling + Google Maps navigation ]
SchedulingBook directly in-app with calendar integration — navigate to the meeting spot via Google Maps without leaving.
[ Q&A board ]
Community Q&ASafe, open discussion board — anyone can ask and answer, similar to Reddit.

🧠 Key Decisions

  • Compatibility score on discovery. Inspired by job matching and dating apps — reduces decision fatigue and makes mentor discovery feel personalized rather than random browsing.
  • Calendar integration as primary action. Scheduling was identified as a key friction point in interviews, so booking was surfaced prominently rather than buried in settings.
  • Q&A board as community layer. Asynchronous connection means mentors with limited time can still contribute — the board scales in ways 1:1 scheduling can't.
  • WHO/WHAT/WHERE framework. Used this upfront to define scope and clarify mentor/mentee needs before touching the interface.

🤝 Collaboration

Responded to a formal Google design challenge brief. Conducted interviews with university students to identify pain points — real feedback validated and iterated design decisions throughout the 6-day sprint.

📊 Impact

Results
  • Complete end-to-end app experience designed in 6 days for a competitive Google challenge
  • Covered user research, journey mapping, user flows, wireframes, and hi-fi screens — within a tight timeframe
  • Demonstrated ability to move fast under structured constraints while keeping accessibility and inclusivity central

📖 Learning

Time constraints force real prioritization — knowing what to cut is just as important a design skill as knowing what to build.
