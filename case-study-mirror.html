Mirror: At-Home Shopping

A next-generation OS for at-home online shoppers — 3D body scanning, virtual try-on, and context-aware outfit recommendations, all in a smart mirror form factor.

UI Designer·Cinema 4D · After Effects3 weeks

🎯 Problem

Online clothes shopping fails at the most fundamental step: users can't know if something fits or looks right before buying. Manual size entry is inaccurate, and the current virtual try-on tech feels disconnected from the actual shopping moment.

💡 Solution

A smart mirror OS that scans the user's body in 3D, enables virtual try-on with accurate fit prediction, and surfaces outfit recommendations based on weather and events — making the shopping experience proactive and precise.

🧠 Key Decisions

  • Mirror form factor. The most natural place for trying on clothes — the design fits into an existing daily routine rather than requiring a new behavior.
  • 3D body scanning. Eliminates manual size entry and guesswork — removes the core friction that drives online returns.
  • Context-aware outfit recommendations. Weather and event-based suggestions make the system proactive, not reactive — increasing utility beyond try-on.

📊 Impact

Results
  • Conceptualized and prototyped a next-generation retail experience from scratch in 3 weeks
  • Demonstrated motion design and UI skills across Cinema 4D and After Effects
  • Full system concept — 3D scanning to real-time recommendations — without a predefined brief

📖 Learning

Designing without a brief reveals your instincts — this project clarified how strongly I gravitate toward the intersection of motion, data, and UI.
